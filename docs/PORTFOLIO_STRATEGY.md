# Applied Data Science portfolio strategy

## Positioning

Applied Data Science with ML, forecasting, multivariate analysis, ETL and visualization. Risk is a relevant application context. Finance/economics expertise and trading quant positioning are not claimed. The degree is in progress.

The first screen identifies the role, personal contributions, academic standing and routes to selected work/CV. The rest of the page connects capabilities to inspectable projects.

## Four-section narrative

1. Hero and Why Me: direct positioning and an open capability matrix.
2. Projects: four asymmetric spotlights, then the complete searchable archive.
3. Experience and Credentials: applied project work, teaching, leadership, degree in progress and proof links.
4. Contact: email link, optional copy action, LinkedIn, GitHub and CV.

Four spotlights: Gammafest, DEKA, FinSight and Coffee Shop. These demonstrate competitive collaboration, stakeholder-facing ETL, multidisciplinary analytics and independent analysis. Commodity forecasting leads the archive and is linked from the first capability row. Spatial and survival work remain available without a standalone econometrics skill category.

## Claim discipline

Each case study states problem, my contribution, team responsibilities, method, findings, limitations and available evidence. Team methods are not personal expertise by association. No invented business impact, measured-savings claims without timing evidence, incomparable metric reductions or generic production-grade labels.

The Gammafest award can be shown with certificate evidence. The conflicting score comparison remains withheld. DEKA's approximate manual-work estimate is explicitly unmeasured. FinSight does not imply ownership of every financial model.

## Visual and interaction decisions

Open editorial sections, real project imagery, restrained asymmetric project spans, Newsreader/DM Sans, graphite surfaces and a single cyan interactive accent. No carousel required to discover projects. No skill-modal detour, nested case-study modal or interactive simulation widget.

Case studies have direct URLs. Bilingual UI/summaries launch with explicit English fallbacks for unreviewed long-form material. All projects remain in the archive, regardless of external repository availability.

## Validation

The 30-second scan is a design hypothesis, not a measured recruiter behavior claim. When readers are available, ask them to identify the intended role, two projects, a personal contribution and a next action after 30 seconds. Do not imply a hiring conversion increase from visual improvements alone.

See the implementation plan for schedule, release checks, evidence questions and safe defaults.


## User-directed interface refinement, 2026-10-20

The current design leads with the personal name and three intersections: Data Science & ML, Statistics & Risk, BI & Data Systems. A self-rated toolkit precedes the three category cards with subskills. All numeric self-ratings were supplied by Alden; they are not an independent assessment.

The selected cards are consistently sized. The archive uses five category-card filters (All projects, the three intersections, Others) and horizontally scrolling image cards. Experience excludes academic collaborations; teaching and organizational responsibilities remain. Experience and credential evidence also use horizontal rails. These explicit preferences supersede earlier asymmetry, open-matrix and non-carousel recommendations.
